# Launch playbook (how repos actually get stars)

The artifact is necessary but not sufficient. Distribution is the other half.

1. **Polish the first screen.** People decide in ~10s. The headline + first
   table are 80% of conversions. Done here — keep them tight.
2. **Ship one real example per claim.** Skeptics star proof, not promises.
3. **Post where the audience is, with a story not a link:**
   - X/Twitter: a thread — "LLMs lie to be helpful. I wrote a prompt that stops it.
     Before/after 👇" then the table screenshots.
   - Reddit: r/LocalLLaMA, r/PromptEngineering, r/ClaudeAI, r/grok — lead with the
     problem, link last.
   - Hacker News: "Show HN: RIGOR — a prompt that makes LLMs say 'I don't know'."
   - LinkedIn if your network is technical.
4. **Time it:** Tue–Thu, morning US time, tends to do best.
5. **Engage fast** the first 2 hours — reply to every comment; early momentum
   compounds the algorithm.
6. **Iterate from feedback** and post "v2 — you asked, here's X." Repeat exposure
   without being spammy.
7. **Add a tuning file when a new model drops** — free relevance + re-share hook.

Honest expectation: most repos get <50 stars; a few get thousands. Quality raises
your odds, distribution raises them more, luck does the rest. Ship, share, iterate.
